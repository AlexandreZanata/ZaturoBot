ZaturoBot includes a small subset as local Android VectorDrawable resources.
Modifications: SVG containers were converted to Android VectorDrawable XML;
the original path data is unchanged, the SVG negative-Y origin is represented
by a +960 Y translation, dimensions are 24dp, and the single-color fill defaults
to white so native views can apply state-specific tint. Resource names were
adapted to this application. These icons are separate from the owner's ZaturoBot
brand mark and are not a Google or YouTube brand logo.

The pinned source revision, per-icon URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
provenance.json. These assets do not require an icon font, network request,
Google service, or Material Components runtime dependency.
