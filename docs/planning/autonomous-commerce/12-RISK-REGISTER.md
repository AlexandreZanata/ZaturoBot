# 12 — Risk register and release decisions

Status: proposed controls. Severity reflects potential impact, not measured incident probability. The owner is accountable for business/channel/purpose authorization; the implementation maintainer owns technical gates. None of the mitigations below is claimed implemented by writing this plan.

## Production blockers

- **R01 — Platform eligibility (critical).** A callable API or an owner account does not authorize unattended commercial posting. Evidence: [official-source matrix](07-PLATFORM-COMPLIANCE.md). Owner records account/medium/link permissions; maintainer denies unknown combinations. Release gate: one unambiguous allowed route and valid standing grant. Fallback: draft/export or a separately approved owned content surface.
- **R02 — Experimental WhatsApp promotion (critical).** MeuAssistente schema/runtime permits fictitious-only synthetic use, with an explicit no-Meta-API decision. Do not reinterpret an allowlist as production consent or remove synthetic checks. Gate: a separately accepted eligible communication architecture and scoped owner authorization; no such authorization is created here. First pilot excludes this channel.
- **R03 — Duplicate external effects (critical).** Crash after send but before receipt can duplicate posts/messages or charged jobs. Persist stable operation identity and send boundary; cache receipts; status-only reconcile. Where the platform cannot prove the result, quarantine and stop automatic resend. Gate: restart/drop-response fixture causes one send at most. Residual: an already published post may need owner-directed correction; no exactly-once promise.
- **R04 — Provider-purpose and privacy mismatch (critical).** Current Go PublicCoding and Console Personal grants are not commerce grants. Go coding traffic expectations and dated privacy agreements are not durable marketing permission. Gate: approved purpose/data/model/region/terms/retention/price profile, denial tests and preserved old routes. Use deterministic templates until it exists. Recheck the dated DeepSeek retention arrangement before expiry, not after a silent rollover.
- **R05 — Spending beyond budget (critical).** Zaturo token limits do not create a monetary ceiling; separate processes may each spend one shared balance. Add fixed-point reserve-before-send against one authoritative payer envelope and keep unknown charges. Preserve MeuAssistente's cumulative USD 10 ledger and current Zaturo debt. Gate: concurrent boundary/overflow/restart/clock tests. Missing price data denies paid work.
- **R06 — Permission and stop races (critical).** Stale queued work, offline workers or human takeover can outlive revocation. Check grant/consent revisions at admission and dispatch with a serialized local fence and short-lived execution authority. Gate: each executor acknowledges its durable local restrictive fence before a global stop is declared effective; Hub acceptance alone is StopRequested/Pending. No new send begins at an executor after its local fence acknowledges. An unreachable executor remains pending; permit expiry alone is not proof that no call is active. Already transmitted requests remain uncertain/observable. Emergency stop must not falsely promise deletion of posts already remote.
- **R07 — False offers or misleading disclosure (high).** Stale variant prices, invented discounts, physical-test claims or hidden affiliation damage trust. Require immutable evidence, timestamps/expiry, brand-pinned disclosures and preflight revalidation. Gate: stale/missing/changed facts cannot dispatch. Owner resolves rights or category exceptions; no model can self-attest testing.
- **R08 — Personal-data exposure (high).** Contacts, conversation bodies, tokens and screenshots may leak through prompts/logs/reports. Keep secrets worker-local, collect minimal purpose-bound audience data, use aggregate reporting, redact errors and implement withdrawal/deletion/backup policy. Gate: synthetic PII canaries absent from Hub/provider payloads and logs; legal basis and retention chosen before personal data ingestion.

## Delivery and operational risks

- **R09 — Cross-repository coupling (high).** Direct SQLite access, shared dashboard login or parallel task queues create competing authority. Use scoped authenticated versioned contracts; pin payload/revision hashes. Gate: wrong role/account/version and changed idempotency payload fail. Existing restaurant tools need a reviewed affiliate adapter, not relabeled orders.
- **R10 — Concurrent development collision (high).** Original main is dirty with computer-use work. The plan branch starts at `9456053`; its schema numbers and capability set are a baseline. Re-audit merged interfaces and migration IDs before implementation. Never reset/stash/rewrite the original checkout. Gate: documentation-only diff and isolated target directory; no original source write by this task.
- **R11 — Resource exhaustion (high).** Browser/video/local inference can exhaust a modest notebook or 1 GB Hub. Use one bounded publisher, small batches and independent media units; no heavy runtime on VPS. Gate: measured RSS/latency under capped offline workload, disk/quota alarms and no starvation of diagnostics. Skip media for MVP. Free space was already limited during planning validation; do not clean unrelated files to make builds fit.
- **R12 — Copyright/identity abuse (high).** Brand templates or marketplace assets may lack commercial rights or imply endorsement. Pin license/provenance/approved brand revisions; check logo/name/media before activation. Gate: every published asset has rights evidence and permitted channel rendition. Zaturo's identity is not automatic permission to brand external accounts.
- **R13 — Metrics and revenue confusion (medium).** Clicks, message interest, estimated commissions and pending conversions can be mistaken for settled revenue. Use source-specific import identities, revisions, currencies, windows and settlement states. Gate: duplicate/refund/cancellation fixtures reconcile without inflating totals; unknown remains unknown.
- **R14 — Time and backlog errors (medium).** UTC offsets are not timezone recurrence. Use IANA rules, injected clocks, occurrence uniqueness and explicit missed-window policy. Gate: local-midnight, ambiguous/nonexistent time, clock rollback, downtime and expiry tests; do not catch up bursts of stale offers.
- **R15 — Credential lifecycle (high).** Credential theft or expiry can block/redefine account access. Dedicated scoped service keys, private transport, local secret references and verified account identity; no credential copies in Hub/plan. Gate: rotation/revocation tests and safe disabled state. OAuth consent is interactive; no profile migration or lock bypass.
- **R16 — Poor commercial return (medium).** A correct bot may produce no attributable revenue. Start with one niche, useful content, one permitted surface and capped spend; report costs/outcomes without guarantees. Owner continues/stops after measured pilot. Do not scale based on model forecasts.
- **R17 — Incomplete public policy access (high).** Some Mercado Livre terms/sharing pages returned 403 and Shopee API details were not publicly verified. Preserve that uncertainty and obtain official account-specific documentation. Gate stays closed; technical inference or third-party articles cannot replace it.
