# 02 — Product vision

Status: proposed. Depends on the [audit](01-REPOSITORY-AUDIT.md), [platform gates](07-PLATFORM-COMPLIANCE.md) and [autonomy contract](06-AUTONOMY-AND-POLICY-MATRIX.md).

## Outcome and operating boundary

Build a small digital curation agency operated from the existing Hub. Its recurring loop discovers eligible opportunities, verifies facts, creates useful original content in an approved identity, publishes in expressly authorized places, observes attributable outcomes, and reports them to the owner. The owner connects accounts, chooses niche/audience, approves a brand profile, sets hard budgets and standing scopes, and handles exceptions. Those setup acts do not require per-post approval once a bounded Level 3 grant exists.

“Independent module” means a separately testable commerce boundary that can be disabled without disabling diagnostics, files, native chat or YoutubeBot. It does not mean an independent permission authority, copied LLM client, another task engine or a second hub. The system must remain useful with every model and external publisher disabled.

Commerce autonomy does not authorize opening social accounts, accepting platform contracts, spending money, changing passwords, using browser sessions, sending messages or publishing in this execution. Existing broad future roadmap entries remain future entries.

## Initial configuration and identity

One AgencyProfile selects niche, languages, audience, allowed product categories, prohibited claims, price range, editorial cadence, channel/account IDs, attribution source, retention profile and reporting window. Recommend affordable technology as a first candidate because products can be explained from documented specifications; owner choice remains required. Exclude medical advice, investment promises and unverifiable performance claims from the first pilot.

A versioned BrandKit contains approved display name, positioning, tone, logo/avatar assets, palette with contrast results, licensed font references, spacing and safe-area rules, banner/thumbnail/post templates, disclosure placement, alt-text rules and per-platform renditions. Store asset hashes, origin/license evidence, dimensions and owner acceptance. Brand changes create a new immutable revision. Scheduled content pins a revision; changing it invalidates affected approvals rather than silently altering a queued post. Do not copy Zaturo's internal product identity onto an unrelated public account without a choice by the owner.

The first brand deliverable is a reviewable kit plus three sample posts using fixture facts. Generate raster visuals only through an eligible separately authorized provider; initial templates can be deterministic SVG/HTML rendered locally. This planning pass generates no actual public identity or social account. Platform name availability, trademarks and font/media rights need checking before adoption.

## Narrow MVP and route to first commerce

MVP-D (offline draft milestone): configure one niche and brand, import five synthetic or explicitly owner-provided product records, validate two fact-backed candidates, render three editorial drafts, and produce one durable Hub result with rejected candidates and reasons. No scraping, model call, link creation, publication, real message or commission claim is needed to prove this flow.

MVP-P (first real assisted pilot): owner enrolls an eligible affiliate account, supplies official links and source evidence, selects one permitted channel and approves one small campaign. Generate a content packet with timestamped price/availability, correct disclosures and publication instructions. The owner publishes manually and records the external post reference. Import only an authorized affiliate performance report; absent data remains unknown. This is a functional assisted commercial operation, not proof of automatic publishing.

MVP-A (first bounded unattended pilot): add one official publisher after provider eligibility and all production gates pass. An owned Telegram channel is only a candidate if the affiliate program explicitly permits that distribution. An owned site is another candidate after hosting/link-policy approval; no site is built here. Start at one campaign, one account, one scheduled post per day and no direct messages. The owner may increase these proposed defaults through a new grant. If no channel qualifies, remain at MVP-P.

## Revenue and acquisition priorities

1. A single Shopee or Mercado Livre affiliate program with verified enrollment, eligible products, official links and an approved channel. Choose based on actual terms/data access, not projected commission headlines. Closed messaging communities may be ineligible for a program.
2. Original educational technology content on an owned channel/site; useful buying criteria create the audience before monetization. Link to verified offers when allowed.
3. Consented offer communities and price alerts, only after explicit channel eligibility, enrollment and unsubscribe handling. Never collect third-party group contacts.
4. Wider cross-platform production after single-channel recovery and measurement gates pass.
5. Creator SaaS after repeatable operation is demonstrated. Multi-tenant identity, billing and per-tenant permissions are outside the single-owner MVP.

The funnel is research → useful content → voluntary audience → explicitly joined community → evidence-backed recommendation → official affiliate link → verified performance report. Each transition preserves platform and consent constraints. Content may be useful without an affiliate offer. Ranking must disclose sponsorship influence and never equate commission with product quality.

## Reports and success criteria

The Hub receives a concise pt-BR summary plus typed evidence: period/timezone, campaign, drafts created, approved/scheduled/published counts, confirmed external IDs, rejected/stale offers, authorized clicks, verified conversions, settled commissions by currency, provider cost, pending charges, uncertainty and next exceptions. Report zero only when a measured source says zero; missing conversions or costs remain unknown. A successful upload or click is not revenue. Pending and settled commissions remain separate; returns/cancellations can revise totals.

Pilot acceptance requires all published content to pin facts, BrandKit revision, grant revision and disclosure, no duplicate effect after an injected restart, no spending beyond reserved caps, and no unresolved external effect automatically repeated. Business success is owner-defined after measured traffic; do not invent ROI, income guarantees or an arbitrary conversion target before data exists.
